# TS-160 — E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982)

**Escape Hatch Episode:** 318  
**Air Date:** August 7, 2026  
**Topic:** Dee Wallace horror résumé — find the film she wasn't in  
**True Answer:** Pumpkinhead (1988) — she was NOT in it  
**Real Films:** The Hills Have Eyes (1977), The Howling (1981), Cujo (1983), Critters (1986)

---

ET gives us Dee Wallace as Mary — the warm, slightly overwhelmed suburban mom at the center of one of the greatest films ever made. What Spielberg knew, and what the audience sensed, was that Wallace brought genuine weight to that role. She'd earned it. Because in the years just before ET, Dee Wallace was surviving things considerably worse than a lost alien in the tool shed.

For today's Truthsayer, five horror films from Dee Wallace's résumé — four are real, one is fiction. Find the film she wasn't in.

Time for the Truthsayer.

**#1** — The Hills Have Eyes (1977) — Lynne Wood, a vacationing mother whose family breaks down in the Nevada desert and finds something terrible waiting.

**#2** — Pumpkinhead (1988) — Ruth Highfield, a tight-lipped rural shopkeeper who warns a grieving father that summoning the demon will cost him everything.

**#3** — The Howling (1981) — Karen White, a television journalist sent to recover at a mountain retreat populated entirely by werewolves.

**#4** — Cujo (1983) — Donna Trenton, trapped in a broken-down car with her son while a rabid Saint Bernard circles outside.

**#5** — Critters (1986) — Helen Brown, a Kansas farm mother whose quiet life is interrupted by carnivorous alien furballs.

Alright — which of these five horror films did Dee Wallace not actually appear in?

It's #2 — Pumpkinhead. She wasn't in it. But she absolutely should have been.

The woman survived cannibals, werewolves, a rabid Saint Bernard, and alien furballs — then turned around and played the most comforting mom in science fiction history. Dee Wallace contains multitudes.

Here ends the Truthsayer.
