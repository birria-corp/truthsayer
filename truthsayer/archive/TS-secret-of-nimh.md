# TS — The Secret of NIMH (1982)

**Escape Hatch Episode:** 310  
**Air Date:** June 5, 2026  
**Topic:** Dom DeLuise and Burt Reynolds friendship  
**True Story:** Don Bluth hired DeLuise specifically to stop Burt Reynolds from using a terrible self-invented dog voice in All Dogs Go to Heaven. DeLuise sat next to Reynolds in the recording booth, and when Reynolds launched into the voice, said "What the hell is that? You're embarrassing me. Just be Burt." It worked.  
**False Stories:** Reynolds bet DeLuise $500 he couldn't finish a full Italian dinner before the next scene (DeLuise won); when Reynolds was hospitalized in 1984, DeLuise wrote a eulogy — Reynolds later demanded it be used at his actual funeral; Reynolds outlived Dom by nine years

---

The Secret of NIMH gives us Dom DeLuise as Jeremy the Crow — all flapping chaos and good intentions, somehow the most lovable disaster in the film. Which is pretty much Dom DeLuise in every film he ever made.

For today's Truthsayer, three stories about Dom DeLuise and his legendary friendship with Burt Reynolds. Two are fiction. One is true.

Time for the Truthsayer.

**#1** — During filming of The Cannonball Run, Reynolds bet DeLuise $500 that he couldn't finish a full Italian dinner his mother had shipped from Brooklyn before the next scene was called. DeLuise won with twelve minutes to spare. Reynolds claimed the bet was fixed because DeLuise had pre-loosened his belt before sitting down.

**#2** — When Reynolds was cast in All Dogs Go to Heaven, he invented what he called his "wonderful dog voice" for the role. Don Bluth thought it was awful. So he called DeLuise, sat him next to Reynolds in the recording booth, and when Reynolds launched into the voice, DeLuise turned to him and said "What the hell is that? You're embarrassing me. Just be Burt." It worked. That's why Charlie the dog sounds exactly like Burt Reynolds.

**#3** — When Reynolds was hospitalized for a serious health scare in 1984, DeLuise quietly wrote a eulogy just in case. Reynolds found out years later and asked to read it. He told DeLuise it was the funniest thing he had ever read and demanded it be delivered at his actual funeral regardless. Reynolds outlived DeLuise by nine years.

Alright — which of these Dom DeLuise and Burt Reynolds stories is true?

It's #2.

Don Bluth confirmed the story himself — he hired DeLuise specifically to save the movie from Reynolds' dog voice. One perfectly timed line from his best friend in a recording booth, and Burt Reynolds just became Burt Reynolds again.

Reynolds outlived Dom by nine years. At Dom's funeral he said "there will never be another like him. I never heard him say an unkind word about anyone."

Somehow that sounds exactly like Jeremy the Crow.

Here ends the Truthsayer.
