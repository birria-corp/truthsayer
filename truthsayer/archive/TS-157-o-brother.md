# TS-157 — O Brother, Where Art Thou? (2000)

**Escape Hatch Episode:** 315  
**Air Date:** July 17, 2026  
**Topic:** Tim Blake Nelson before Hollywood  
**True Story:** Classics major at Brown; delivered Senior Orator address entirely in Latin at 1986 commencement  
**False Stories:** Family oil and gas business in Tulsa; orthopedic surgery residency at Johns Hopkins

---

O Brother Where Art Thou gives us Tim Blake Nelson as Delmar — sweet, credulous, and absolutely convinced he has been washed clean of his life of Sin. It is a perfect performance from a wonderful actor, and very interesting man.

For today's Truthsayer, three stories about Tim Blake Nelson before Hollywood found him. Two are fiction. One is true.

Time for the Truthsayer.

**#1** — After graduating from college, Nelson spent eighteen months working in his family's oil and gas business in Tulsa before his father sat him down and told him he was the worst businessman he had ever seen and should go do something else immediately.

**#2** — Nelson was a Classics major at Brown University, and in 1986 delivered the Senior Orator address at commencement entirely in Latin. He was the last Brown student to do so.

**#3** — After Brown, Nelson enrolled in orthopedic surgery at Johns Hopkins, completing enough of his residency to assist on several hip and knee replacements before walking away to pursue acting full time.

Alright — which of these Tim Blake Nelson stories is true?

It's #2.

Tim Blake Nelson addressed his entire graduating class in classical Latin. Tim has credited his foundational studies in Latin — which he began in middle school and pursued throughout college — with providing an underlying structure that organizes his critical thinking and informs how he writes and directs films. Sounds just like Delmar.
