# TS-161 — The Dark Knight (2008)

**Escape Hatch Episode:** ~319  
**Air Date:** ~August 2026  
**Topic:** Michael Caine's lean years before fame  
**True Story:** Vidal Sassoon cut Michael Caine's hair before either man was famous  
**False Stories:** Pierre Cardin suit; nude modeling for David Hockney life-drawing class

---

Michael Caine's Alfred is the steadiest hand in Gotham — the guy who's seen everything and never loses his composure. Long before that, young Caine was broke, unknown, and living hand-to-mouth in London with roommate Terence Stamp, years before either one mattered to anybody.

Three stories about Michael Caine's lean years before fame. Two are false, one is true.

Time for the Truthsayer.

**#1** — Desperate for a sharper look ahead of a big audition, Caine had a suit made by an up-and-coming Parisian designer named Pierre Cardin, years before Cardin became a household name.

**#2** — Caine's regular barber during those broke years was a young stylist named Vidal Sassoon, long before Sassoon became the most famous name in hair.

**#3** — To make rent, Caine posed nude for a life-drawing class taught by a then-unknown art student named David Hockney.

Alright — which of these three stories is true?

It's #2. Vidal Sassoon cut Michael Caine's hair in London years before either man was anybody. Caine got the trims. Sassoon got the practice. History got a haircut.

Somewhere in an alternate timeline, Caine's walking around in a Cardin original with a Hockney sketch of himself hanging in a gallery. In this one, he just had good hair.

Here ends the Truthsayer.
