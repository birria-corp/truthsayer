# TS — Almost Famous (2000)

**Escape Hatch Episode:** 308  
**Air Date:** May 24, 2026  
**Topic:** Lester Bangs' New York years  
**True Story:** On April Fool's Day 1979, Bangs and Mickey Leigh (Joey Ramone's brother) snuck into Electric Lady Studios with a renovation key; recorded an album as Birdland; cassette became the master recording, released 1986  
**False Stories:** Shoving match with Dictators singer at CBGB led to cab-sharing friendship; contrarian Springsteen pan killed by Creem editor

---

Almost Famous is Cameron Crowe's love letter to the moment before everything gets complicated. And at the center of it, dispensing wisdom from a payphone like a prophet is Lester Bangs — One of my favorite Phillip Seymour Hoffman roles. Lester Bangs coined the term punk rock before punk rock existed and spent his career being exactly right about everything while nobody paid him enough.

For today's Truthsayer, three stories from Lester Bangs' New York years. Two are fiction. One is true.

Time for the Truthsayer.

**#1** — After a chaotic night at CBGB in 1978, Bangs got into a shoving match with the lead singer of The Dictators over a review. They both got thrown out, shared a cab uptown, and became close friends, so much so, Lester dedicated his Blondie biography to him.

**#2** — On April Fool's Day 1979, Bangs and Mickey Leigh — Joey Ramone's brother — snuck into Electric Lady Studios with a renovation key for a late night session. The cassette from that night became the master recording, released in 1986.

**#3** — When Born to Run dropped, Bangs filed a pan to Creem calling Springsteen "a very good car salesman in a leather jacket." His editor killed it. Bangs never wrote about Springsteen again on principle.

Alright — which of these Lester Bangs stories is true?

It's #2.

Bangs and Mickey Leigh snuck into Electric Lady — built by Hendrix. Zeppelin recorded there. Bowie too — and they recorded some demos in the dark of night.

The only true currency in this bankrupt world is what you share with someone else when you're uncool. Lester Bangs knew that. He had the tapes to prove it.

Here ends the Truthsayer.
